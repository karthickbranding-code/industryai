The problem

General-purpose robots need armies of engineers to stay running.

A model trained in winter fails in summer. A model trained for Product A fails for Product B. Every new scenario means a new engineering cycle.

You end up needing as many AI engineers as robots — wiping out the labour savings that justified the deployment.

Our answer

Domain-specialised agents.

Pre-trained on hundreds of millions of domain-specific simulation hours, each agent arrives already knowing its domain’s physics, failure modes and success patterns.

It fine-tunes on your environment during supervised onboarding — then keeps improving from operational experience.

WHY WE BUILT THIS

The first deployment succeeds because engineers pay enormous attention to it. The second and third fail. We built agents that ship themselves — not just the first time.
